## Start with the Hub

**Dwarfium Hub** is the front door: download it once, and it installs, updates and starts the other two for you — your data is never touched by an update. It also keeps your Dwarfs and your computers (home PC, laptop, mini PC at a remote site) in step.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Discover the Hub](apps/hub.md)

!!! tip "Search tips"
    The search looks into every page. The docs are in English, like the apps' default language: search with the English word (*mosaic*, not *mosaïque*).
